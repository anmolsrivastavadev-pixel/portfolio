About

Products people can use, not just demos.

I take ideas from a blank file to a live product that people can download, sign up for, or open in a browser. I care about the unglamorous parts too: data you can trust, privacy by default, and interfaces that explain themselves.

Lately that has meant an AI trends tracker that reads more than a dozen sources every 30 minutes, a market research tool that grounds every idea in real complaints, a Mac AI companion, and an Android app that verifies every file before anything gets deleted.

I'm a student at Redland Green School in Bristol, and I'm planning to study AI, machine learning and computer science at university. Until then I learn by shipping: picking a real problem, building the product, and putting it in front of people.
